Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you start on my farm?+

After an initial consultation call, we can conduct a field assessment within 3–5 working days. For urgent requests such as a crop pest outbreak or irrigation failure, we prioritise same-day or next-day visits.

What is the minimum farm size you work with?+

We work with farms as small as half an acre. We believe every farmer deserves access to quality agricultural services regardless of their land size. Our pricing is structured to be fair for small and large holdings alike.

Do you offer services outside Tamil Nadu?+

Yes — we currently operate across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana. We are expanding to Maharashtra and Gujarat in 2025. Remote consultations via video call are available nationwide.

How much does a soil analysis test cost?+

A standard NPK + pH soil test starts from ₹499 per sample. A comprehensive micronutrient panel (12 parameters) is ₹1,499. For farms above 10 acres, we offer discounted multi-sample packages. Contact us for a custom quote.

Is Stackly Agriculture certified or government-recognised?+

Yes. We are empanelled with the Tamil Nadu Agriculture Department and NABARD. Our soil lab is NABL-accredited, and our agronomists are certified by the National Institute of Agricultural Extension Management (MANAGE).
